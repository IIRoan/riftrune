# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Riftbound collectors and players using the app on mobile at shops, events, and trade tables, or on desktop while sorting binders, checking prices, and comparing printings. They need to find the right card fast, understand the exact version, and update ownership without ambiguity.

## Product Purpose

The Astral Grove is a card catalog and collection tracker. It should make card discovery, card details, variant comparison, price checks, and collection updates feel fast, trustworthy, and consistent across mobile and web.

## Brand Personality

Instrument-panel precision. The app is a terminal war room for collection work: stark black canvas, monochrome chrome, and one bright figure when hierarchy needs to land. Calm authority comes from contrast, spacing, and a clear Lato weight ladder (Regular body, SemiBold structure, Bold commit) — not soft tint stacks, glow, or game UI chrome. Card art, rarity marks, and live price signals carry personality; controls stay quiet and mechanical.

## Visual world

**Factory** — deep black control surface where light panels and chalk CTAs land like flashlit dispatch. See [`DESIGN.md`](./DESIGN.md). Chromatic color is reserved for live data and status (trends, ownership completeness, keyword badges), never for button fills or decorative chrome.

## Anti-references

- Gamer neon, gold/champagne outlined pills, soft `primary/12` tint CTAs as the default action language
- Noisy card-game chrome, glow, blur, and drop-shadow elevation
- Generic SaaS dashboards, cream editorial stacks, broadsheet hairline layouts
- Marketing-first screens that block catalog work
- All-caps marketing type stacks or weight remaps that hide Regular body; chromatic brand fills on primary actions
- Theatrical motion — prefer short mechanical state switches

## Design Principles

Start in the task: search, browse, inspect, and update collection state should be visible immediately.

Collector confidence: prices, printings, rarity, set, ownership, and auth state need clear hierarchy and stable formatting.

Version clarity: when a user acts on a specific printing, the app should preserve that exact printing instead of asking again.

Depth through figure/ground: `#eeeeee`-class surfaces on `#101010`-class canvas (and chalk CTAs on dark) beat soft elevation.

Smooth restraint: motion is 150–200ms mechanical switches for state only; reduced-motion supported.

Personality through artifacts: real card imagery, game symbols, and precise microcopy — not decorative effects on chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Support keyboard focus, readable text sizes, clear hit targets, reduced-motion preferences, and color-independent status labels for rarity, ownership, auth, and market states. Functional accents (signal / metric) never carry meaning alone.
